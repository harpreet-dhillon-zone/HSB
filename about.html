Our Story

Built on craft, not compromise.

HSB began as a small family workshop with a simple ambition: to install stone the right way. Years later, that ambition hasn't changed — but the workshop has.

Today we're a tight team of fabricators, templaters and installers serving homeowners, designers and contractors across England. Every project — whether a single bathroom vanity or a full residential fit-out — gets the same hands-on attention.

We've never grown into the kind of business that hands a project off to a sub-contractor. The person who measures your space is the person who'll install your stone.
